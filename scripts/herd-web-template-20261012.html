Astra's PetOS as a 3D map: every pet, model, program and piece of infrastructure, and what flows between them. Built from pets.json and herd-web.json. Click a sphere or a line for details.

How to use it

Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom. Click a sphere for its job and every connection; click a line for everything that flows both ways between its two ends. Moving dots show which way information flows. Herd-wide links (pets that reach every other pet) stay hidden until you switch them on or click one of those pets.
