Every pet and what it talks to, grouped by role. Distance from the centre shows architecture, not power or trust. The glass marks the intended harness boundary; it is not an operating-system sandbox. Both routes still require PetOS authorization. Lines describe architecture, not live traffic.

ROLES AND ACCESS

You own the system: approve, grant, revoke and recover. Agents need an authenticated identity and an explicit grant for each action and resource. A name, hat, model or position on this map grants nothing.

Security: what is enforced?

Built: checks in participating registry scripts and public-data build checks. Procedural: owner review of core changes. Tested prototype: signed, scoped requests and four independent reviews plus owner approval for sensitive actions. Planned: protected enrollment, hardware-backed owner approval, isolated accounts, durable replay storage and a broker on every execution path. Unverified: effective permissions on this device. This page cannot inspect them.

Moving around (like Unity)

Right-drag to look around; while holding it, W A S D fly, Q/E down and up, Shift faster (the keys also work without the mouse). Scroll zooms toward the centre, or toward what you selected. Scroll-click drag (hold the wheel down) or left-drag spins the sphere freely in any direction, with no stops; Shift + scroll-click drag pans. F frames what you selected (or every pet). On a phone: one finger orbits, two fingers zoom and pan.

How to use it

Click a shape for its job, its ring and every connection; click a line for everything that flows both ways between its two ends. Moving dots show which way information flows. Links to every pet (pets that reach every other pet) stay hidden until you switch them on or click one of those pets.
