System Map

Astra's PetOS as a 3D map: every pet, model, program and piece of infrastructure, and what flows between them. Built from pets.json and system-map.json. Shells follow the architecture layers: the neutral registry at the core, the Farmer, every pet agent, the glass AI harness around them, models and programs, and you and your devices outside. Click a node or a line for details.

Every item in the keys below is a switch: click it to hide or show that kind of thing.

Line colour = architecture layer (click to hide)
Line style = how real it is (click to hide)
Shape key (click to hide)
Colour key (nodes, click to hide)
Moving around (like Unity)

Right-drag to look around; while holding it, W A S D fly, Q/E down and up, Shift faster (the keys also work without the mouse). Left-drag spins the sphere freely in any direction, with no stops, middle-drag pans, scroll zooms, F frames what you selected (or every Intelligent App). On a phone: one finger orbits, two fingers zoom and pan.

How to use it

Click a sphere for its job and every connection; click a line for everything that flows both ways between its two ends. Moving dots show which way information flows. Links to every app (pets that reach every other Intelligent App) stay hidden until you switch them on or click one of those pets.
