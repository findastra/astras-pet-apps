`apps/footer-template-20261014.html`. The footer has thin top/bottom rules and a
light surface. Its centered groups wrap with generous spacing and a small vertical
stagger: status, Made by Astra/date, Find Out More/social icons, Take Me Home/static
Farmer icon. Sparkles separate groups. No coins, spinning, hidden links or horizontal
scrollbar. Keep license/language notes on their own background above the signature.

without embedded credentials. Existing approved GitHub, Pole TikTok, VR TikTok and
Farm destinations remain unchanged. Save affects this browser only. Copy embed uses
the public component and needs that branch deployed; export includes source but the
Farmer image still requires its public host. Neither action publishes other sites.
